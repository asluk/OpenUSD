## Completion criteria

Every selected workflow, implementation, audit and delivery step is executed.
There are no deferred in-scope demonstrations or stale collateral. Scope defers
coordinate-epoch representation, interpretation and computation; ordinary datum
and height operations may use engine-selected resources. Unsupported requests
fail visibly. Larger geographic extents are exercised rather than excluded.
Partner controls and synthetic/conditional assumptions are distinguished.
Candidate completeness, execution success and group approval are separate claims.
